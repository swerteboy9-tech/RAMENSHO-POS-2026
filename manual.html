1First time setup (only once)

  1. Open this link on your phone: swerteboy9-tech.github.io/RAMENSHO-POS-2026
  2. Add it to your home screen:
    • Android (Chrome): tap ⋮ (top right) → Add to Home screen
    • iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button → Add to Home Screen
  3. Open the app from the 昇 icon. On 🏠 HOME, choose your own name under “Today's staff”. You only do this once on your phone.
Use your own phone and your own name. Your check-in time and pay come from this.

3Start of shift

Check in

  1. On 🏠 HOME, make sure your name is selected.
  2. Tap CHECK IN. It shows “🟢 On duty since …”.

Open the register (first person of the shift)

If HOME says 🔒 Register is closed, tap it (or 💵 SHIFT) and fill in:

  1. Count the cash in the drawer and enter it. The app shows what the last shift left. If it is different, tell the owner.
  2. Store GCash balance — open the shop GCash app and copy the balance.
  3. Choose the Shift and the Weather.
  4. Count the stock. Numbers are filled in from the last close — change only what is different. For chashu, ground pork, chili oil, green onion and lard, tap Enough / Low / Out.
  5. Tap OPEN REGISTER.
Meal allowance ₱100: after you check in (and the register is open), the app says “Meal allowance ₱100 — take it from the drawer”. Take ₱100 from the drawer. It is already recorded — do not enter it again in EXPENSE.
Open the register only once per shift. If someone already opened it, just check in.

4Taking an order

  1. Tap 🍜 ORDER. Use the tabs (Ramen, B1T1, Don, Side, Drink) to find items.
  2. Tap an item. Add toppings (kaedama, ajitama, extra chashu), a note for kitchen (e.g. “less spicy”), and the quantity. Tap ADD TO ORDER.
  3. When everything is added, tap 🛒 CHECKOUT.
  4. Choose DINE-IN or TAKE-OUT, set the number of Guests, and type the table no. or customer name.
  5. Choose CASH or GCASH. For cash, tap the amount the customer gave — the app shows the change.
  6. Tap COMPLETE ORDER. The order appears on the kitchen screen of every phone.
B1T1 (Buy 1 Take 1) = 2 bowls at a special price (e.g. Tonkotsu × Tonkotsu ₱280 instead of ₱398). It is for dine-in only: both bowls must be eaten in the shop. If even one bowl is taken out, do not use B1T1 — enter the bowls at the normal price as two orders: the bowl eaten here as DINE-IN, the bowl taken home as a separate TAKE-OUT order (it gets the container fee). The app will not allow B1T1 as take-out.
Take-out containers are added automatically: ₱15 for ramen / don / rice, ₱10 for sides.
If the app shows “🍜＋🍚 can be a set — save ₱…”, tell the customer and tap Make set if they agree.
For GCash, check the payment on the shop GCash before you tap COMPLETE ORDER.

5Kitchen screen

  1. Tap 👨‍🍳 KITCHEN. Orders are shown oldest first, with the minutes waiting.
  2. Cook from the top. When the order is given to the customer, tap ✓ SERVED.
  3. A ticket turns red after 15 minutes — serve it first.
  4. Tapped SERVED by mistake? Tap Show served orders → ↩ Undo.
How to cook each bowl: 📖 RECIPES on HOME. Tonkotsu and chashu-men use white noodles; tantanmen uses yellow noodles.

6Sold out items

  1. On 🍜 ORDER, tap ⛔ Mark an item sold out.
  2. Tap the item. It becomes SOLD OUT on every phone. Tap it again when it is back.
  3. Tap ✓ Done marking sold out.

If an item shows a warning that it may be out, the stock count says that ingredient is gone. Check in the kitchen before you take the order.

Always mark sold out items — the owner uses this to know how much we lost and to prepare more next time.

7Buying with register money

Every time money leaves the drawer to buy something, record it — or the cash will not match at closing.

  1. Tap 🧾 EXPENSE.
  2. Tap what you bought (e.g. Eggs 30pcs, Green onion, LPG). If it is not in the list, tap ＋ Enter manually.
  3. Check the Quantity and the Amount (₱) — use the amount on the receipt.
  4. Paid from:
    • 💵 Register cash — taken from the drawer
    • 📱 Store GCash — paid from the shop GCash
    • 👤 Owner / outside — the owner paid, not the register
  5. Tap SAVE EXPENSE. Keep the receipt.

Salary advance / on-call pay

On the EXPENSE screen, tap 💸 Salary advance or 📞 On-call day pay (₱600). Choose who receives it, check the amount, then tap SAVE. The owner will see it in payroll — an advance is deducted on payday.

Pay advances only when the owner says yes.

At 15:00 HOME shows ⏰ 15:00 stock check. Tap it, count, then tap SAVE CHECK. You can also count any time: 📦 STOCK → 🔄 Quick check.

If HOME shows 🧾 To prep / buy today, tell the owner or the next shift right away. Do not wait until it is gone.

10End of shift

  1. Tap CHECK OUT on HOME.
  2. If you opened the register (or you are the last one on duty), the app asks to close the register first. Tap OK.
  3. Count the cash in the drawer. The app shows ✓ Matches, or Over / Short ₱….
  4. Enter the store GCash balance from the GCash app.
  5. Count the stock (same list as opening). The app shows how many there should be.
  6. Cash taken out for the owner / deposit — enter it if you hand money to the owner. The app shows what is left for the next shift.
  7. If cash is over or short, write the reason in Note.
  8. Tap CLOSE REGISTER & CHECK OUT. Read the summary (cash, GCash, stock, to-prep list).
Not matching? First check: an expense not recorded, a GCash sale entered as CASH, or a missed order. Fix it in HISTORY, then tell the owner.

11Fixing mistakes

  • Wrong order: 🕘 HISTORY → tap the day → Edit or Delete.
  • Wrong expense: HISTORY → EXPENSES tab → Edit or Delete.
  • Forgot to enter an order: enter it as usual and change Order date & time on the checkout screen.
  • Forgot to check out yesterday: next time you tap CHECK OUT, the app asks what time you really left. Type it as HH:MM (e.g. 21:30).
  • Wrong stock record: STOCK → “Recorded this shift” → Delete.

12Problems & rules

The sign at the top right

✓ synced 10:52All good — everything is saved to the sheet.
⏳ 3 to sendNo internet right now. Keep working — it sends by itself when the internet is back.

Rules

  • Every sale goes in the POS. No paper notes.
  • Use your own name. Never check in for another person.
  • Do not delete the app or clear the browser data while “⏳ to send” is showing — those records would be lost.
  • Keep every receipt for expenses.
  • Prices and the menu come from the owner. If a price looks wrong, tell the owner — do not change it yourself.
  • If “Two registers are open” appears, close both and count the cash only once.
Menu looks old? 🍜 ORDER → scroll down → 🔄 Refresh menu.
